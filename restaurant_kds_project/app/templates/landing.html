01 — PLATFORM
Welcome

What does Listo do?

A platform built to eliminate friction at every stage of a modern restaurant's service.

Listo unifies the complete operation of a restaurant into a single web system. From the moment a waiter takes an order to when the customer receives their dish, every step flows digitally, instantly, and error-free.

Kitchen Display (KDS)
Orders sent in real time to the kitchen screen with sound alerts via WebSocket. No printed tickets.
Dining Room Module
Waitstaff create and send orders from any device. No paper, no shouting to the kitchen.
Point of Sale
Payments, configurable taxes, multiple payment methods, and integrated sales reports.
Inventory
Automatic stock control when orders are dispatched. Movement history and low-stock alerts.
Real-Time
WebSocket technology: updates reach all connected devices instantly.
Admin Panel
Product and agent management, downloadable reports in CSV, Excel and PDF, and audio settings.
